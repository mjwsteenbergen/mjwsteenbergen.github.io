2024: Loss

There are these moments in your life, where you realise that your life is gonna go in a radically different direction. Moments where your envisioned future shatters and a different one is put in it’s place.

That happened to me November last year. When on a cold and rainy day mom told us she did not want to live anymore and had gotten a euthanasia approval.

A new future is created. Life just kinda.. stops. Any big projects are paused and put away. There are more important things. I had planned on making another big website that year, as I’d done in 2022, but I had other things on my mind. Even this year, I still do not have the energy to make something big, so I’m keeping it small.

After she told us, you continue with life. The day-to-day keeps you grounded, but feels uneasy. I am so happy I got to have time with her. To ask her. To understand. To learn. Slowly it dawns that this is going to happen; it's not just a bad dream. Every out she’s already thought of, this is something that she wants and what can I ask of a mother that gave us everything.

We find time to visit the US. To her brother and dear family. We start to plan her final month. Figure out who she’s still able to see in her very tired state. Both my brother and I get our friends to meet her. She gets to organise her own funeral. We book a vacation home in Zandvoort to get away from it all for one last time. We play minigolf. I lose. But most of all we… have fun. People keep telling me how special it is to have a family unit this close, but to me, it’s always been like this. Conversation flowing, quick quips and lots of laughter.

The day comes. The day she passes. We’ve had Indonesian food the night before. Her favourite. She drives herself to the hospital. Of course she does. We stay strong until an hour until the moment and we turn into blubbering messes. You hug her. It ends.

With her final moments, she saves five people, donating her organs to people who need them. An angel in life and death.

Me and my brother stay close and with friends for the week. The funeral is beautiful. The reception is in a cafe. Mom didn’t want it to be sad. Her ashes are spread out over the sea, close to the same place her dad was, so now every time you’re close to any sea, you’ll be close to her.

And then you have no more things you have to do. I spent an entire month going around to people important to me and my mom. Talking with them, about mom, about our loss helped a lot. I slowly start to get back into work.

Then the next step hits. It no longer starts to be “new” to your coworkers and friends. They start to move on from it. And thats both good and bad. Loss is not a cold you get rid of. It stays for a long time, but at the same time it is also good to not get reminded about it the entire day. It gets tiring after the 5th “how are you?”.

That does not mean it is not greatly appreciated. If you’ve reached out this year, texted me asking me how I’ve been, sent me a card or if you spent time with me drinking a coffee or tea, listening and being there in any way, I can’t tell you how much that has meant to me and how grateful I am to have you in my life. I won’t ever forget it.

Once I started back up working again, I took up a big project at work that was close to my heart. And you find yourself in the next big step. That something new gets so big that it “pushes” the loss out of the way. There is too much to think about and you suddenly realise that you haven’t thought about your mom for a couple of days. You're realizing you're slowly starting to move on.

Although by design nothing else much happened this year, other things happened as well. Getting to visit friends in Derby, getting to care for the neighbours cats (who is honestly the cutest), karaoke with my old team, the goodbye with my old team, who all dressed up in red for the occasion, going to Alicante, going ice skating and almost falling multiple times, multiple amazing concerts and dressing up as Pete for Sinterklaas at the office for fun and giggles.

Now, at Christmas time, there is once again a moment where her loss is felt immensely. The house is not decorated, we did not add Christmas lights to our frontyard tree, which was always a staple of the festive period. But mostly, its her soul, her openness, her true interest and caring in you that I’d love to have right now.

Be good, be careful, be safe, be happy

Martijn
